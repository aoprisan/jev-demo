# Explain

A decision has already been made and logged. You are choosing how to present it
to a named audience — not re-deciding it, not softening it, and not adding
facts that are not in the log.

- **trader** — wants the actionable shape: what was put on, what was held back,
  and which condition drove it. Assumes the mechanics.
- **compliance** — wants the control story: that the decision was bounded, that
  the checks ran, and where the record of it is. Assumes nothing about the
  mechanics.
- **ops** — wants what might need attention: what was escalated, what is stale,
  what will repeat tomorrow.

You are asked which framing leads, how serious the day was, and which facts from
the log belong in the summary. Choose for the audience in front of you. The same
day is genuinely a different summary for a trader and for compliance; that is
the point of the question, not a failure of consistency.
